How it works

From screen to subtitle in three steps

No game files touched, no mods, no hooks. The app simply watches the region you choose and translates what appears there.

STEP · SELECT

Draw a region

Drag a box over the dialogue area, subtitles, menus, or anywhere text appears in your game.

STEP · READ

On-device OCR reads it

A fast neural OCR engine recognizes the text directly on your PC. Your screen never leaves your computer.

STEP · PLAY

Translation overlays the game

A clean, transparent, always-on-top box shows the translation right where you're looking. Keep playing.

Privacy Policy

Real Time Game Translator — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Overview

Real Time Game Translator ("the app") is designed to work without collecting personal data. Text recognition (OCR) runs entirely on your computer. We do not require an account, we do not run our own servers for translation, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone.

2. What stays on your device

  • Screen captures of the region you select are processed in memory, on your PC, and are never uploaded or saved by the app.
  • All app settings, game profiles and preferences are stored locally on your computer.
  • Your API keys (DeepL, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure) are stored locally and are only used to call the translation engine you selected.

3. What leaves your device

The only data that leaves your machine is the recognized text, which is sent to the translation engine you chose so it can be translated — exactly the same as typing that text into a translator yourself. This text is transmitted directly from your PC to the provider; it does not pass through our servers.

4. Third-party translation engines

When you use an online translation engine, the recognized text is processed by that provider under its own privacy policy. Depending on your choice, this may be DeepL, Google, or Microsoft. Please review the privacy policy of the engine you select. If you use an on-device engine, no text leaves your computer at all.

5. Purchases & licensing

The app is distributed through the Microsoft Store. Purchases and Pro license restoration are handled entirely by Microsoft and are tied to your Microsoft account. We do not receive your payment details; Microsoft processes purchases under its own privacy statement.

6. Analytics & tracking

The app contains no ads, no tracking SDKs and no built-in analytics. The Microsoft Store may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as install counts), which contain no personal information.

7. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Since no data is collected, no special parental action is required.

8. Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles data, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above before the change takes effect.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at odforge@hotmail.com.
